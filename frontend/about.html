About EzTends

Making Tender Management Easier

A digital platform designed to connect Tendering Authorities and Suppliers across Sri Lanka.

Who We Are

A Better Way to Manage Tenders

EzTends is a Sri Lankan web-based tender management platform that helps organisations publish tender opportunities and allows registered Suppliers to find and submit bids through one central system.

Our aim is to make tender activities easier to manage, improve access to business opportunities, and provide a clear process for both Tendering Authorities and Suppliers.

Our Purpose

To provide a simple and reliable digital environment where Sri Lankan organisations and Suppliers can manage tender activities efficiently.

Our Platform

What EzTends Provides

01

Tender Opportunities

Suppliers can search available tenders, view tender information and find suitable business opportunities.

02

Online Bid Submission

Registered Suppliers can submit their bid amount and proposal documents directly through the EzTends system.

03

Tender Management

Tendering Authorities can create, update, monitor and close their tender opportunities from their account.

04

Bid Review

Tendering Authorities can review received bids and select a successful Supplier after the tender is closed.

05

Document Management

Suppliers can upload supporting documents while Administrators can review and validate submitted documents.

06

Reports & Monitoring

EzTends provides tender, bid and user information to help authorised users monitor activities in the system.

Simple Process

How EzTends Works

1

Register

Create an account as a Supplier or Tendering Authority.

2

Publish or Search

Authorities publish tenders while Suppliers search for opportunities.

3

Submit & Review

Suppliers submit bids and Authorities review received proposals.

4

Award

The successful Supplier can be selected after the tender closes.
