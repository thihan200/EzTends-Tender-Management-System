How EzTends Works

The system supports the main tender process from publishing an opportunity to selecting the successful supplier.

01

Publish a Tender

A Tendering Authority creates a tender with its category, budget, dates and required documents.

02

Submit a Bid

Suppliers browse open tenders, submit their bid amount and upload the required proposal documents.

03

Select a Supplier

The Tendering Authority reviews submitted bids, selects a supplier and marks the tender as awarded.
